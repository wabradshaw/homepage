ABOUT ME

I grew up in Durham, England before moving up to Scotland to read software engineering at the University of Edinburgh. My focus there was on Natural Language Generation, building AI the hard way, before LLMs became a thing.

Since then I've led teams across a mix of startups, scaleups, and even TikTok to solve problems for customers. I have a particular focus on AI, testing, and complex business logic.

When I'm not working, you can usually find me building things, travelling, and forgetting to update this site.
